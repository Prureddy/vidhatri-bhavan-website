Our story

Vidhatri Bhavan is a pure vegetarian family hotel run by the Vidhatri Group of Hotels in Shivamogga, with branches in Gandhinagar and Gopala Gowda Extension.

Our day begins at 6:30 in the morning with soft idlis, crisp vadas and strong filter coffee, and the chutney still comes in little bronze cups. By lunch the banana leaves are out for full meals, and the kitchen keeps going till 10:30 at night with North Indian thalis, biryanis and Chinese favourites.

Our promise is right there on our logo: quality is our strength. Good food, honest portions, and prices a whole family can enjoy together.
